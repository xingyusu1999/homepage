About Me

I am a PhD student in the group of Prof. Matsuda at the University of Tokyo. My research focuses on ultrafast light–matter interaction, nonlinear optics, and soft X-ray spectroscopy using free-electron lasers (FELs).

I am particularly interested in element-specific ultrafast dynamics, including soft X-ray second-harmonic generation (SXSHG) and XMOKE, with applications to magnetic materials and semiconductor systems.
