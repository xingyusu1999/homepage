Exploring ultrafast light–matter interaction using soft X-ray free-electron lasers (FELs), with a focus on nonlinear spectroscopy, magnetization dynamics, and element-resolved physics.

About

I am a PhD student in Prof. Matsuda’s group at the University of Tokyo. My research focuses on nonlinear X-ray optics and ultrafast dynamics in magnetic and semiconductor systems.

Research Focus

  • Soft X-ray Second-Harmonic Generation (SXSHG)
  • Ultrafast magnetization dynamics (XMOKE)
  • FEL-based spectroscopy (SACLA)
  • Nonlinear optics in semiconductors (GaAs, InAs)
