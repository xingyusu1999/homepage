About Me

Hello! I am Xing Su, a PhD student in Prof. Matsuda’s group at the University of Tokyo.

My research focuses on ultrafast light–matter interaction, nonlinear optics, and soft X-ray spectroscopy using free-electron lasers (FELs).

I am particularly interested in element-specific ultrafast dynamics, including soft X-ray second-harmonic generation (SXSHG) and XMOKE, with applications to magnetic materials and semiconductor systems.
